The Decision in Front of Us

Three models, one question

A
Off and awayGrades K–8, full day. Phones off rather than silenced, stored in a locker or cubby, and not carried at transitions, lunch, or recess
B
App-managedThe device stays with the student. Parents set emergency contact rules; the district disables apps, social media, and camera during school hours
C
Bell to bell in classGrades 6–8. Devices in caddies or backpacks every period, with passing periods, lunch, and recess also restricted
All three answer where the phone goes. None answers what happens on the next screen.

Read the Definition

What the policy reaches, and what it exempts

Regulated

The personal device

Phones, smartwatches, and earbuds. Stored for the day, app-managed, or held during instruction, depending on the model a district adopts.

Exempt by definition

The school-issued device

“Wireless communication device” does not include a device issued by the school or a teacher for educational purposes.

ISBE’s own model policy reports teachers estimating one in three students on laptops for nonacademic work.

The Next Device

Then the glasses arrived

A parent at dismissal, apparently wearing recording-capable glasses. The next device walked in through the pickup line.

The wearer

A visitor, not a student. Lockers, caddies, and a parent app reach none of it.

The law

Not the device statute. The Eavesdropping Act turns on a private conversation, and a car line is not a classroom.

The remedy

No category ban. Prohibiting eyewear would prohibit accessibility with it.

Regulate the conduct, not the eyewear.
At the roadwayDisable recording functions during arrival and dismissal.
At the doorNo capture in nonpublic areas without authorization. Accessibility devices permitted.
InsideKeep recording, transcription, and AI capture off.

The Leadership Challenge

Restriction creates a boundary.
Leadership, design, and partnership create change.

Our work is not only to manage devices. It is to design environments in which technology serves learning.

BoundariesSet clear expectations for attention, device presence, and capture.
