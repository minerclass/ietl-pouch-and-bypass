Read the Definition

What the policy reaches, and what it exempts

Regulated

The personal device

Phones, smartwatches, and earbuds. Stored for the day, app-managed, or held during instruction, depending on the model a district adopts.

Exempt by definition

The school-issued device

“Wireless communication device” does not include a device issued by the school or a teacher for educational purposes.

ISBE’s own model policy reports teachers estimating one in three students on laptops for nonacademic work.

The Leadership Challenge

Restriction creates a boundary.
Leadership, design, and partnership create change.

Our work is not only to manage devices. It is to design environments in which technology serves learning.
